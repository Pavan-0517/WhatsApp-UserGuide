# Installing WhatsApp on Desktop

**Option 1: WhatsApp Web**

1. Open [whatsapp](https://web.whatsapp.com).  
2. On your phone, open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices.  
3. Tap **Link a Device** and scan the QR code.  

**Option 2: WhatsApp Desktop App**

1. Download WhatsApp Desktop from [whatsapp](https://www.whatsapp.com/download).  
2. Install and open the app.  
3. Scan the QR code using your phone (Linked Devices).  
4. Start messaging from your computer.

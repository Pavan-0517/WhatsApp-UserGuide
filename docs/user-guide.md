# WhatsApp User Guide

- **Messaging**: Send text, emojis, GIFs, images, videos, documents, and voice notes.  
- **Calls**: Make free voice and video calls over Wi-Fi or mobile data.  
- **Groups**: Create groups with up to 1024 members.  
- **Status**: Share photos, text, or 30-second videos that disappear after 24 hours.  
- **Media Limits**:  
  - Images: Up to 16 MB.  
  - Videos: Up to 16 MB (~90 seconds to 3 minutes depending on resolution).  
  - Documents: Up to 100 MB.  
- **Payments (UPI in India)**: Send and receive money securely via WhatsApp Pay.

If you face issues, check the [Troubleshooting Guide](/docs/troubleshooting.md).  

For account safety, review [Best Practices](/docs/best-practices.md).

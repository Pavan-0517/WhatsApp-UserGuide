# Installing WhatsApp on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store**.  
2. Search for **WhatsApp Messenger**.  
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to complete.  
4. Open the app and tap **Agree & Continue**.  
5. Enter your phone number and verify with the *OTP*.    
6. Set your profile name and photo.
> **Note**: Restore chats from **Google Drive** (optional).

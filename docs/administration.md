# WhatsApp Administration

- **Group Management**: Add/remove members, assign admins, edit group info.  
- **Privacy Controls**: Manage who can see your profile photo, status, and last seen.  
- **Security**: Enable two-step verification for added account protection.  
- **Backup**: Schedule automatic backups to Google Drive (Android) or iCloud (iOS).  
- **Linked Devices**: Manage multiple devices connected to your account.

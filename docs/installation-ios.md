# Installing WhatsApp on iOS (iPhone)

1. Open the **App Store**.  
2. Search for **WhatsApp Messenger**.  
3. Tap **Get** → **Install**.  
4. Launch WhatsApp and tap **Agree & Continue**.  
5. Enter your phone number and verify with the OTP.  
6. Set your profile name and photo.

>**Note**: Restore chats from **iCloud** (optional).

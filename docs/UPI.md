# WhatsApp UPI & Payments

WhatsApp supports Unified Payments Interface (UPI) in India, allowing users to send and receive money securely.

## Setting Up WhatsApp Pay

1. step 1: Open WhatsApp → Tap **More options** (⋮) → **Payments**.
1. Step 2: Tap **Add Payment Method**.
1. Step 3: Select your bank from the list.
1. Step 4: Verify your phone number linked to the bank account.
1. Step 5: Set up your UPI PIN (if not already created).

### Sending Money

1. Open a chat with the recipient.
2. Tap the **₹ / Attach** icon → **Payment**.
3. Enter the amount and add a note (optional).
4. Confirm with your UPI PIN.

### Receiving Money

- The sender initiates the payment, and you’ll receive a notification once funds are transferred.

### Security

- End-to-end encryption ensures transaction safety.
- UPI PIN is required for every transaction.
- WhatsApp does not store your bank details.
